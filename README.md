# Mirage Blade: Hourglass

**60 Minutes on the Golden Hourglass. Six Torchlit Palace Chapters. One Gleaming Scimitar.**

`Mirage Blade: Hourglass` (`com.ngd.mirageblade`) is a native 120Hz Metal/SpriteKit kinematic action-platformer and tactical scimitar-dueling game for **macOS 14.0+**, **iOS 16.0+**, and **iPadOS 16.0+** inspired by the legendary 1989 cinematic dungeon classic — built from scratch with 100% original IP, procedural artwork, and dual-chip Arabian Hijaz / 1989 AdLib FM synthesis.

## Highlights
- **6 Multi-Room Palace & Catacomb Stages**: *The Sunken Dungeon*, *Catacombs of Bone*, *Moonlit Courtyard*, *Hall of the Mirror*, *Shadow Light Spire*, and *Vizier's Hourglass*.
- **Tactical Scimitar Fencing**: Timed Steel Parries (`Up / LEAP`) trigger golden spark bursts and open double-damage **Riposte** counter-strikes against Palace Captains, Immortal Skeletons, and Grand Vizier Zafar.
- **Classic 1989 Dungeon Traps & Potions**: Loose crumbling limestone slabs, pressure-plate portcullis gates, concealed floor spikes (safe when sneaking!), slicing steel guillotine jaws, Ruby/Golden/Emerald elixirs, and the Silver Magic Mirror.
- **Live 1989 ↔ 2026 Time-Machine (`V` & `B` / `ERA` & `CHIP`)**: Toggle anytime between 120Hz HD Arabian Nights art + Oud/Darbuka synth and authentic 16-color 1989 EGA pixel art + AdLib OPL2 FM audio.
